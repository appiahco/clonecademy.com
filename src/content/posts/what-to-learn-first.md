The most common question we get from founders is which role to take first. Our answer is always the same: pick the role you'd otherwise hire first.

## For most founders, that's frontend

If you don't have a technical cofounder, the first thing you'll pay someone for is almost always the part customers see: a landing page, a sign-up flow, a prototype you can put in front of people. Frontend is also the role with the shortest loop between an idea and a result. You change a line, save, and see it.

After 16 weeks in the Frontend role, you can build and test ideas on your own, and you can read and review what a contractor ships.

## When to start somewhere else

- **Backend**, if your product is mostly rules and data: a marketplace, a fintech tool, anything with payments or permissions at its core.
- **Cloud**, if you already have a product and the bill, the outages or the deploys are what keep you up at night.
- **AI/ML**, if your pitch depends on a model working, and you need to know whether it actually does.

## You can take all four

Roles have no prerequisites, and you can take one per cohort in any order. Plenty of founders come back with the same team, swapping seats each time, until everyone on the team understands the whole product.
