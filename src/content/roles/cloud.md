Cloud engineers own where the product runs and how it gets there. On a cohort team you turn a laptop project into a production system on AWS, defined in Terraform so every change is reviewed and repeatable.

For founders, this role takes the mystery out of the cloud bill. You'll know what you're paying for, why, and what to change when traffic arrives.
