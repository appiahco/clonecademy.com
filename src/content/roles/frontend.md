Frontend engineers own everything a customer sees. On a cohort team you turn the product's screens into a working Next.js web app and an Expo mobile app, and you're the first to find out when the API contract doesn't match what the screen needs.

For founders, this is the role that makes you fastest at testing ideas: after 16 weeks you can put a new landing page, onboarding flow or prototype in front of customers without waiting on anyone.
